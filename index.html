Why now

Skill education is now part of every CBSE school year

From the 2026–27 session, CBSE schools teach skill education from Class 6, and computational thinking and AI from Class 3. Students need somewhere to build what they read about.

Grades 6–8 · Middle stage

NCERT's Kaushal Bodh

  • Mandatory in CBSE schools from 2026–27
  • Nine projects across Grades 6–8, three a year
  • Hands-on, project-based learning

Grades 9–12 · Secondary stage

NCERT's Kaushal Vikas

  • Mandatory skill subject for Class 9 from 2026–27
  • 100-mark Class 10 board exam: 50 theory, 50 practical
  • Builds on Kaushal Bodh with in-depth vocations

Most schools still lack the labs, kits and trained teachers. VINC brings the hands-on side under one roof.

Kaushal Bodh and Kaushal Vikas are NCERT textbooks. VINC is an independent provider and is not affiliated with NCERT or CBSE. Sources: NCERT textbooks; CBSE skill education circulars.

Questions parents ask

Before you visit

Is this tuition or coaching?

No. VINC is a skills studio. Students learn by building projects with a mentor, and progress is shown through a portfolio and a skills report card, not marks.

Is VINC part of CBSE or NCERT?

No. VINC is an independent provider. Building and Applied AI are our own programmes. They complement the skill education your child's school now teaches, including NCERT's Kaushal Bodh and Kaushal Vikas.

How big are the batches?

25 students per batch. Each programme runs two weekend batches every quarter, so the centre has 100 weekend seats a quarter.

Which programme is right for my child?

Grades 6–8 join Building. Grades 9–12 join Applied AI. If you're unsure, take the free skill assessment or book a free demo class first.

What does my child take home?

A documented project portfolio, a skills report card each quarter and a verified skill certificate. They also present their work at the quarter-end showcase.

Is the online LMS really free?

Yes. Theory modules and practice quizzes are free for every student with a parent's consent. Labs, mentoring, projects and certificates happen at the centre.

When are fees paid?

At enrolment, before the quarter starts. Building is ₹18,000 and Applied AI is ₹24,000 per student for the programme.

Can our company sponsor seats?

Yes. Companies can fund scholarship seats from their CSR budgets. Send us an enquiry and we'll share the details.
